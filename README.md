**I build analytics designed to make decisions or make the right decision obvious.**

Whether it's a sales leader deciding where to target next, or a customer deciding what to buy, most of the friction isn't a lack of data — it's too much of it, presented wrong, or not validated before someone trusts it. I design and build the pipelines, metrics, and reporting that close that gap.

I'm especially drawn to sales and marketing analytics — program-designed reporting that directly informs planning, targeting, and direction-setting, not just after-the-fact dashboards. I also spend a fair amount of time thinking about financial behavior data (spending patterns say a lot about a person or a business before they say it themselves), which is part of what's pulling me toward performance management work in fintech.

## How I work

- **Validate before optimizing** — a metric gets checked for whether it's actually right before I build anything on top of it.
- **Hypothesis-driven** — state the assumption, test it, measure the result, decide whether to iterate. Not one-and-done analysis.
- **Root cause over symptom** — find the process or rule actually driving the number, not just the number itself.
- **Own the pipeline, not just the build** — governance and cadence over time, not a one-time deliverable.
